A few helpful details

Before you request.

What comes in my sample?

We select the product and quantity with you based on your project, buying volume, and availability. A request does not guarantee every product or a full retail bag.

Is shipping included?

The approved sample product is free. We confirm any shipping or delivery charge before fulfillment. Larger pallet and bulk trials have separate trucking costs.

When will it ship?

We coordinate fulfillment after reviewing your request. Our team will confirm timing and provide tracking when your sample is shipped.

Can I request a commercial trial?

Yes. Tell us your typical volume and application. Pallet and bulk trials are reviewed individually, with product availability, unloading access, and freight confirmed by the team.
