Organic Soil Wholesale · Size Category Guide
by Soil Seed & Water Group · V5 Local Pricing (All Products) · format guide
How to Choose the Right Format
These size categories help customers compare storage, handling, loading, and delivery needs before discounts are applied.
Format Best Use Handling Notes
9 lb sealable bag
Entry size, resealable
 Good for retail shelves, samples, small customer jobs, and easy repeat purchases. Easy to count, store, move by hand, and keep closed between uses.
1 CF, 1.5 CF, and 2 CF bags
White bag formats, usually 40-50 lb
 Good when the customer needs a movable single unit with more product than the 9 lb bag. Strong for storage. The bag helps hold water and moisture so product still has wetting when opened.
Pallet
Same bag format, full pallet volume
 Good for retailers, contractors, nurseries, one-off projects, and one-time projects. Easier to count, ship, store, and move with a forklift or pallet jack.
Tote / super sack
2.2 cu yd, movable bulk
 Better pricing than bagged pallets while still movable. Better for storage than loose bulk when product must be moved later. Can usually load at least one unit into a heavy-duty pickup or trailer.
Attention: customer needs a way to unload. Forklift is best. Manual unloading is possible but not recommended. Product can dry out because the sack breathes and loses moisture within weeks.
Pickup by cubic yard
Local loader pickup
 Best for customers who can receive loose material, coordinate pickup, and use the product soon. Requires coordination and loader availability. Confirm location, product, loader, bucket size, and whether pricing is by cubic yard or by ton.
Truckload flatbed
Usually around 22 pallet spots
 Best for palletized orders, tote orders, mixed loads, equipment, and palletized bulk products. Often comes with a Moffett forklift. The Moffett has big tires and can usually handle dirt yards better than a standard warehouse forklift.
Walking floor loose truckload
Loose material, usually priced by ton
 Best for big-volume jobs, farms, yards, landscapers, repeat bulk fills, and customers who can receive a large loose pile. Needs open space for truck access, turning, and unloading. If not used quickly, cover with a tarp or compost tarp to manage rain, drying, and moisture.
