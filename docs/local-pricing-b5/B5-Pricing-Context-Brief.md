## Core Pricing Logic

- Keep selling units clear: primary price first, alternate unit or list price in muted text.
- Light-product conversion for potting soil and mulch: `24 tons ≈ 60 cu yd` → `1 ton ≈ 2.5 cu yd`, `1 cu yd ≈ 0.4 ton`.
- Moisture, compression, density, and yards are estimates, not perfect constants.
- Heavier products (dairy compost, worm castings, wet material) may still quote more by tonnage.

## Size Categories

- 9 lb sealable bag: entry size, easy to store, reseal, sample, and retail.
- 1 CF, 1.5 CF, and 2 CF bags: movable single-unit bags, usually 40-50 lb.
- Pallet: same bag format in full pallet volume; sheet shows 10% off list.
- Tote / super sack: better pricing than bagged pallets while still movable.
- Pickup by cubic yard: local loader pickup (Congress or Phoenix); B5 raises light-product pickup.
- Truckload flatbed: usually around 22 pallet spots; full load +10% off story.
- Walking floor loose truckload: loose material for big-volume jobs.
